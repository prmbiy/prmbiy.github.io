I am a Research Fellow at [MATS](https://www.matsprogram.org/){:target="_blank"} and work with [Proximal](https://www.proximal.ai/){:target="_blank"} on AI4Code, Formal Methods, reward hacking, benchmarking and evals.

My research focuses on how AI systems can write correct and verifiable code, and reason with human-like flexibility. 
I work at the intersection of **AI4Code**, **AI4Math**, **Formal Methods**, and **Human-AI Interaction**. 
Recently, I developed [SpecGuard](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09159){:target="_blank"}, which uses Lean 4 to detect and formally certify conflicts between coding-task descriptions and tests before an agent acts.

Previously, I worked at Microsoft Research's [PROSE team](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/group/prose/){:target="_blank"} on autonomous coding agents, conversational debugging, and rubric-based evaluation, with contributions deployed in Visual Studio and GitHub Copilot. A recurring thread of interest in my research is translating informal intent into machine-checkable specifications. I started with mathematical autoformalization in Lean 4, then into verified code generation and, at Qualcomm, autoformalizing natural-language requirements for semiconductor design verification.

Outside of research, I like to swim, dive, read manga, and play grand strategy video games.

Check out my [CV]({{ '/assets/resume_param_biyani_oct_2026.pdf' | relative_url }}){:target="_blank"}, or reach me through [email](mailto:parambiyani8@gmail.com){:target="_blank"}.
